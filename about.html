About Me

I am Kazuha Mo, a Computer Science student at UC Irvine who enjoys technology, the web, and building practical things that people can actually use. I like exploring how simple ideas can become useful tools, clean interfaces, and small projects that make everyday tasks easier.

This website is my personal space on the web. It is where I share a bit about myself, experiment with ideas, and gradually collect the projects and utilities I care about. I prefer work that feels straightforward, thoughtful, and quietly useful rather than overly complicated.

Outside of classes and technical work, I am also interested in personal projects, online services, and creating spaces that feel organized and meaningful. Over time, this site will continue to grow into a home for my tools, experiments, and things I want to keep building.
