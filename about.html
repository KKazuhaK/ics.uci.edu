Bio

I enjoy technology, the web, and building practical things people actually use. I like exploring how simple ideas become useful tools, clean interfaces, and small projects that make everyday tasks easier.

This site is my personal space on the web — where I share a bit about myself, experiment with ideas, and collect the projects I care about. I prefer work that feels straightforward, thoughtful, and quietly useful.
