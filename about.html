About Me

Kazuha Mo

B.S. Computer Science UC Irvine kazuhak@uci.edu

I enjoy technology, the web, and building practical things that people can actually use. I like exploring how simple ideas can become useful tools, clean interfaces, and small projects that make everyday tasks easier.

This website is my personal space on the web — where I share a bit about myself, experiment with ideas, and gradually collect the projects and utilities I care about. I prefer work that feels straightforward, thoughtful, and quietly useful.

Outside of classes and technical work, I am also interested in personal projects, online services, and creating spaces that feel organized and meaningful. Over time, this site will continue to grow into a home for my tools, experiments, and things I want to keep building.
